## Tips for Posting:

1. **Best Time**: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 12-1 PM (your local time)
2. **Engagement**: Respond to every comment in the first 2 hours
3. **Hashtags**: LinkedIn allows up to 30, but 5-10 is optimal
4. **Media**: Add screenshots or a demo GIF for higher engagement
5. **Follow-up**: Post updates when you hit milestones (100 stars, new features, etc.)
